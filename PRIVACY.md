# Privacy Policy: Cloudflare R2 Storage for Frappe

_Last updated: 26 October 2026 · Publisher: Supagigs · Contact: support@supagigs.com_

**Summary: Supagigs does not collect, receive or store any of your data through this app.**

## What the app does with data
- The app runs entirely inside your own Frappe / ERPNext site.
- Files you upload are sent **directly from your site to your own Cloudflare R2 bucket** (and, if you migrate, read from your own Amazon S3 bucket). They never pass through Supagigs servers.
- Your R2 and S3 credentials are stored **encrypted in your own site's database** (Frappe Password fields). They are never shared with Supagigs or anyone else.
- The app contains no analytics, telemetry, tracking or "phone home" calls.

## Third parties you choose to use
Your files are stored with Cloudflare (and Amazon Web Services if you use the S3 migration) under your own account and their privacy terms:
- Cloudflare: https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/
- AWS: https://aws.amazon.com/privacy/

If you run on Frappe Cloud, Frappe's own privacy policy also applies to your site: https://frappe.io/privacy

## Support requests
If you email support@supagigs.com, we use your email and whatever you send only to answer you. We never ask for your API keys. Please do not send them.

Changes to this policy are published in this file, with the date at the top.
